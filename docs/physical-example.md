# Physical example

This page shows a physical Washer Punch39 workflow from printed jig to assembled washer stack.

## 1. Printed punching jig

![Printed Washer Punch39 punching jig](../images/washer-punch39-printed-jig.jpg)

Print the full-scale punching jig at 100% / actual size and verify the scale before use.

## 2. Jig aligned on the washer

![Washer Punch39 jig aligned on a washer](../images/washer-punch39-jig-alignment.jpg)

The paper jig is aligned over the washer and held in place before punching through the paper.

## 3. Punched washer

![Punched Washer Punch39 washer](../images/washer-punch39-punched-washer.jpg)

After the jig is removed, the punched marks can be visually inspected on the washer face.

## 4. Assembled stack

![Washer Punch39 washer stack assembled with a bolt and nut](../images/washer-punch39-assembled-stack.jpg)

The reference implementation can be stacked on a bolt and secured with a nut for storage.

For dimensions, encoding rules, and recovery instructions, see the main [README](../README.md) and the other documents in this directory.
